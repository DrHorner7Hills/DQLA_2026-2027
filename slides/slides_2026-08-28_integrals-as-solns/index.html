Examples

Newton’s law of cooling

The time rate of change of the temperature of a body is proportional to the difference between the body’s temperature and the temperature of the surrounding medium.

Torricelli’s law

The time rate of change of the volume of water draining from a tank is proportional to the square root of the depth of water in the tank.

We can find a general equation and also a common special case of a right cylindrical tank.

Population growth

A simplistic model of population growth when birth and death rates are constant is that the time rate of change of the population is proportional to the current population.

If we further know that the population at 0 hours is 1000 bacteria and that the population at 1 hour is 2000 bacteria, how can we make the differential equation and its solution more specific to this particular scenario?

In-class practice

IC-1

Verify by substitution that both \(y_1=\cos(x) -\cos(2x)\) and \(y_2=\sin(x)-\cos(2x)\) are solutions to the differential equation \(y^{\prime\prime}+y=3\cos(2x)\text{.}\)

IC-2

For what values of \(r\) is \(y=e^{r x}\) a solution of \(4y^{\prime\prime}=y\text{?}\)

IC-3

Write a differential equation describing a graph whose tangent to the graph at point \((x,y)\) intercepts the \(x\)-axis at the point \(\left(\frac{x}{2},0\right)\text{.}\)

IC-4

Write a differential equation modeling the information that the acceleration \(\frac{d v}{d t}\) of a Lamborghini is proportional to the difference between 250km/h and the velocity of the car.
